## Character card · <name>

Copy this section for each character.

- **Role**: what function they serve in gameplay (tutorial guide / shop / story key …)
- **Personality in three words**: (pick three words, ideally with tension between them for depth)
- **Speech style**: verbal tics, sentence length, honorific habits, things they would never say — **this field directly determines whether AI-written dialogue sounds right**
- **What they want**: the character's desire/struggle (the engine that drives plot)
- **Relationships**: which characters they connect to, and the nature of the connection
- **Visual anchors**: key features for art/AI illustration (hair color, build, signature items)
- **Off-limits**: things this character would never do
